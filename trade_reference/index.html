The Trade Ledger

Every unmodified vanilla Bedrock villager profession, plus the Wandering Trader, tier by tier, straight from Mojang's own trade files. Built as a lookup for designing this pack's own trade nerfs and restores — not narrative content.

How to read this

  • Each row is one trade: what the villager wants from you, what it gives back, and how many times that exact trade can be used before it needs to level up again.
  • A "picks N of the following M" row means the villager only ever offers N of those M options at once, chosen at random when it takes the job — not all of them simultaneously. Plain trades directly under a tier heading are always offered.
  • "1 of: ..." inside a cell means that single trade slot resolves randomly to one of the listed items (e.g. a random wool color) rather than being a pick against other trades.
  • Cartographer's biome-locked map trades (village_taiga, swamp_hut, village_snowy, village_savanna, village_plains, jungle_temple, village_desert) are further restricted to cartographers born in specific biomes — no cartographer ever sells a map back to its own home biome. See the season planner's Trade Web note for the exact biome-to-biome table.
  • A couple of item names still carry a legacy Bedrock data-value suffix vanilla's own files haven't cleaned up (e.g. potion (data 8)) — left as-is rather than guessed at, where the exact variant isn't confidently known.
